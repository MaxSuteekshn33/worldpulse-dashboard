world-terrain.webp
------------------
NASA Blue Marble Next Generation with topography and bathymetry (December
2004), 5400x2700, downscaled to 3200x1600 and encoded as WebP at q68.

Source: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73909
NASA imagery is in the public domain.

Equirectangular (plate carree), -180..180 longitude by -90..90 latitude, which
is the same frame lib/outlines.ts uses — so the loading screen can place the
image with no projection step.
