world-terrain.webp
------------------
NASA Blue Marble Next Generation with topography and bathymetry (December
2004), 5400x2700, downscaled to 4608x2304 and encoded as WebP at q66.

Source: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73909
NASA imagery is in the public domain.

Equirectangular (plate carree), -180..180 longitude by -90..90 latitude. The
globe view samples it directly in that frame, one source pixel per screen
pixel of the disc, so no projection step is stored anywhere.
